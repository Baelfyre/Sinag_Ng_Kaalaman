## General rule

A third-party source may be useful for research without being eligible for inclusion in the production question bank.

When third-party material is incorporated, preserve:

- source identity
- license or conditions of use
- required attribution
- commercial-use restrictions
- ShareAlike requirements
- modification notices where required

If a third-party license conflicts with the default Sinag license for a particular file or dataset, the third-party license controls for that third-party material.
